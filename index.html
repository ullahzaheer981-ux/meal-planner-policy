Privacy Policy for Meal Planner

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Meal Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Meal Planner Android application.

Information We May Collect

Meal Planner may allow you to enter information such as your name, age, gender, height, weight, activity level, calorie goals, meal preferences, and other information used to provide meal-planning and nutrition features.

Information entered into the app may be stored locally on your device depending on the feature you use.

Nutrition Information

The app provides general nutrition information for foods and meals. This information is provided for general informational purposes and should not be considered medical advice.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Meal Planner may send reminders such as meal and water reminders. You can disable notifications through your device settings.

Advertising

Meal Planner may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services to provide advertising, analytics, or other functionality. These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Meal Planner is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Meal Planner developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
